all-mo-diary

A diary with one entry a day: change the font size, bold and colour, add images, and find past entries again with the calendar, filters and search.

Your diary stays with you

  • Your diaries and images are stored only on your phone, or in your own Google Drive when you turn on cloud features.
  • Export files are fully encrypted and can only be imported with the Google account that created them. We never have the key.
  • No ads and no analytics tools.
